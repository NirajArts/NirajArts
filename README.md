## 🎯 About Me

I'm a **Lead XR Developer, 3D artist, and indie game developer** who loves turning imaginative ideas into interactive experiences.

- 🧭 **7 years of professional experience**, starting in **3D modeling, rigging, and animation** and growing into **XR development and technical leadership**.
- 🥽 I build **VR/MR experiences, interactive simulations, and immersive training applications**, bringing together real-time development and 3D art.
- 🎮 Outside my professional XR work, I develop **indie games** with a focus on inventive mechanics, puzzles, and sci-fi worlds.
- 🛠️ I enjoy creating **reusable Unity systems and tools** that make complex interactions easier to build.
- 🚀 I'm curious about emerging technologies, including **AI-assisted creative workflows and Web3 gaming**.

## 🔨 What I'm Working On

- **XR development:** Interaction systems, mixed-reality experiences, and hands-on simulations.
- **Indie game development:** Experimental gameplay concepts, prototypes, and worldbuilding.
- **Tools & art:** Unity workflows, real-time 3D assets, and systems that bridge art and engineering.
